Then `meaning_ja__vi` is empty, `confidence` low, and `vi_check_reason` quotes the gloss evidence. This is **not** the same as a false friend: same characters with a shifted meaning (e.g. 自己 tự kỷ, gloss "autistic") is `same_word` and goes to task 2. The prompt must say: `source_vi=composed_attested` values were composed from character readings and their `gloss_vi` may describe an unrelated word (D51); **`gloss_vi` of a `kaikki_en_han` word is English (D53)**, of other words Vietnamese.
