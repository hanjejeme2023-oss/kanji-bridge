| D25 | **Vietnamese orthography:** NFC, lowercase, modern tone placement. Closed syllables keep the tone on the second vowel (toán, hoàn, loạn: do not move it). Apply the same function to every Vietnamese input (Unihan, Kaikki, FrequencyWords) before any comparison or join. | D26 | **`freq_vi`** (rank, 1 = most frequent). Used only for ordering cards inside a level and for VI-only banding; documented as a proxy (`freq_vi_basis=syllable_min` in `coverage_report.csv`). | No licensed multi-syllable frequency list is available; a documented proxy is better than a guess, and the ordering is secondary. |
